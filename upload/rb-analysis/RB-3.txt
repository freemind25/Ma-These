TITLE/TYPE
  TYPES OF RESEARCH GAPS
  Subtitle: "Research gaps highlight what is unknown, unclear, or needs
  further investigation."
  Type: Taxonomy/classification infographic

---

KEY CONCEPTS AND FRAMEWORKS

  SEVEN TYPES OF RESEARCH GAPS (Hierarchical taxonomy):

  TIER 1 — EPISTEMOLOGICAL GAPS (Foundational):

    GAP 1: EVIDENCE GAP (Contradictory Evidence)
      Icon: ⚖️ Scales of Justice
      Definition: Findings across studies are inconsistent or contradict
      each other.

    GAP 2: KNOWLEDGE GAP (Knowledge Void)
      Icon: 📖 Open Book
      Definition: The needed evidence or research on a topic does not
      exist.

    GAP 3: PRACTICE–KNOWLEDGE GAP (Action–Knowledge Conflict)
      Icon: 📋 Clipboard/Checklist
      Definition: There is a difference between what research shows and
      what is done in practice.

  TIER 2 — PROCEDURAL, CONCEPTUAL & CONTEXTUAL GAPS (Advanced):

    GAP 4: METHODOLOGICAL GAP (Method & Research Design)
      Icon: 📊 Bar Chart with Magnifying Glass
      Definition: New methods or research designs are needed to gain new
      insights or reduce bias.

    GAP 5: EMPIRICAL GAP (Evaluation Void Gap)
      Icon: 🔍 Magnifying Glass over Line Graph
      Definition: Existing findings require empirical testing or
      validation.

    GAP 6: THEORETICAL GAP (Theory Application Void)
      Icon: 💡 Light Bulb
      Definition: Existing theories are missing or have not been applied
      to the research problem.

    GAP 7: POPULATION GAP
      Icon: 👥 Group of People Silhouette
      Definition: Certain populations are underrepresented or have not
      been adequately studied.

---

CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

  Three-category meta-classification:
    A. Epistemological Gaps (1-3): State of existing knowledge
       (contradiction, absence, misapplication)
    B. Procedural Gaps (4-5): How knowledge is generated
       (flawed methods, untested findings)
    C. Conceptual/Contextual Gaps (6-7): Theoretical framework or
       demographic scope

  Seven diagnostic criteria for evaluating academic maturity:
    1. Consistency Check (Evidence Gap)
    2. Coverage Analysis (Knowledge Gap)
    3. Implementation Fidelity (Practice-Knowledge Gap)
    4. Methodological Rigor (Methodological Gap)
    5. Validation Status (Empirical Gap)
    6. Theoretical Grounding (Theoretical Gap)
    7. Demographic Representativeness (Population Gap)

DERIVED RULES FOR AI PROMPTS

  Rule 1 — DIAGNOSTIC PROMPT (Identification)
    Template: "Analyze the following research topic: [INSERT TOPIC]. Using

    literature. Justify your choice."

  Rule 2 — GENERATIVE PROMPT (Gap Statement Formulation)
    Template: "I am studying [TOPIC]. Generate three distinct research
    gap statements based on this taxonomy: one focusing on a [Gap Type A],
    one on a [Gap Type B], and one on a [Gap Type C]. For each, draft a
    formal 'Problem Statement' paragraph using academic language."

  Rule 3 — LITERATURE REVIEW AUDIT PROMPT
    Template: "Act as a critical academic reviewer. I will provide
    summaries of five key papers in [FIELD]. For each paper, assess
    whether it contributes to closing a Knowledge Gap, resolving an
    Evidence Gap, or addressing a Population Gap. Identify which gap
    remains most open after reviewing these sources."

  Rule 4 — METHODOLOGY SELECTION PROMPT
    Template: "My research identifies a [e.g., Methodological Gap]
    regarding [SPECIFIC ISSUE]. Recommend three qualitative or quantitative
    research designs specifically suited to address this type of gap."

  Rule 5 — PRACTICE-TO-THEORY TRANSLATION PROMPT (Gap #3 specific)
    Template: "Describe a common practice in [INDUSTRY/FIELD]. Identify
    the 'Practice-Knowledge Gap' by contrasting this real-world action
    with current academic evidence. Propose a research question that
    investigates why this discrepancy exists."

  RULE: Treat the seven gaps as mutually exclusive tags/filters. By
  explicitly naming the gap type, force the AI to adopt a specific
  critical perspective rather than providing generic feedback.
