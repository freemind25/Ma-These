KEY CONCEPTS AND FRAMEWORKS

  1. SIX-SECTION ARCHITECTURE FOR LITERATURE REVIEW (Chapter 2):

     Section 1 — INTRODUCTION
       Function: Restate research problem, objectives, or questions.
               Outline how the chapter is structured.
       Example: "This chapter reviews the existing literature on executive
       loneliness in the workplace. It begins by examining theoretical
       perspectives on solitude, followed by empirical studies on workplace
       well-being, and concludes with the identification of research gaps
       that inform this study."

     Section 2 — THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK
       Function: Present and explain theories guiding the research.
               Justify why these frameworks are suitable.
       Example: Uses Job Demands-Resources (JD-R) Model and Social Support
       Theory to frame the study.

     Section 3 — CONCEPTUAL FRAMEWORK
       Function: Define main concepts and how they relate to each other.
               Often illustrated with a diagram.
       Example: Illustrates relationship between executive roles,
       organizational culture, perceived loneliness, and coping strategies.

     Section 4 — REVIEW OF RELATED LITERATURE
       Function: Organize past studies thematically, chronologically, or
               methodologically. Critically analyze what is known and unknown.
       Possible Subsections:
         - Historical Background
         - Current Trends and Debates
         - Empirical Studies (local and international)
         - Methodological Insights (approaches, strengths/limitations)
       Example themes: Loneliness & Leadership, Workplace Mental Health,
       Coping Mechanisms.

     Section 5 — CRITICAL REVIEW AND SYNTHESIS
       Function: Compare and contrast studies. Identify strengths, weaknesses,
               contradictions, and gaps. Show where your study fits.
       Example: Highlights lack of focus on African/Sub-Saharan contexts.

     Section 6 — SUMMARY OF LITERATURE REVIEW
       Function: Pull together key insights. Highlight research gap(s).
               Prepare readers for the next chapter (Methodology).
       Example: Summarizes influences and transitions to methodological
       choices for the next chapter.

  2. THREE ORGANIZATIONAL LOGICS for Section 4:
     - Thematic: Grouping by topic
     - Chronological: Ordering by date of publication
     - Methodological: Grouping by research approach (qualitative vs quantitative)

  3. KEY DISTINCTION:
     - Review = listing studies
     - Critical Review/Synthesis = evaluating studies against each other

---

METHODOLOGIES / CLASSIFICATION SYSTEMS

  - Linear but iterative flow: Introduction → Theory → Concepts →
    Evidence → Synthesis → Conclusion
  - Gap identification as central methodological goal (geographic, cultural,
    methodological gaps)
  - Synthesis vs. Summary as quality criterion for literature reviews

DERIVED RULES FOR AI PROMPTS

  Rule 1 — ROLE & CONTEXT SETTING
    Assign the AI the role of academic researcher; provide the specific
    research topic immediately.
    Template: "Act as an expert academic researcher. My topic is [Insert
    Topic]. Write the Introduction for my Chapter 2..."

  Rule 2 — STRUCTURAL SEGMENTATION
    Do NOT ask for the whole chapter at once. Use the 6 sections as
    separate prompts for depth and adherence.
    Template: "Now, write the Theoretical Framework section. Use [Theory
    Name] and justify its suitability for explaining [Phenomenon]."

  Rule 3 — EXPLICIT ORGANIZATIONAL LOGIC
    When asking for Section 4, specify which method (Thematic,
    Chronological, or Methodological).
    Template: "Provide a Thematic Review of recent literature on [Topic].
    Create subheadings for [Theme A], [Theme B], and [Theme C]."

  Rule 4 — CRITICAL SYNTHESIS INSTRUCTION
    Explicitly instruct AI to compare, contrast, and find contradictions.
    Template: "Write a Critical Review and Synthesis. Compare the strengths
    and weaknesses of these studies. Identify specific contradictions
    regarding [Variable X] and explain where my study fits."

  Rule 5 — GAP IDENTIFICATION FOCUS
    Force the AI to look for what is missing (geography, methodology,
    population).
    Template: "In the Summary, highlight the research gap. Emphasize the
    lack of studies in [Specific Region/Context] regarding [Specific
    Aspect]."

  Rule 6 — BRIDGING FUNCTION
    For the final summary, link the literature review forward to the
    methodology chapter.
    Template: "Conclude with a Summary that transitions into Chapter 3.
    Explain how identified gaps will dictate methodological choices."
