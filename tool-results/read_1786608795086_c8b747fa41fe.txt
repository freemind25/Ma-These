   294→Rewarding, essential, potentially taken for granted and relatively under-theorised, research supervision plays a key role in higher education in empowering
   295→students to become researchers. As both undergraduate and postgraduate
   296→student numbers grow in universities, we are increasingly being invited to
   297→supervise students' research projects, encouraging, supporting and enabling
   298→them to develop skills, values and practices essential to learning the roles
   299→and rules of research. Research is defined here as the crucial element of
   300→learning - a fascination with questioning the world, ways of enquiring,
   301→solving problems, creating and innovating and developing discourse, strategies and interpretations. In this dynamic context, there is a clear need for
   302→sound, supportive and empowering supervision practices for students'
   303→research study, whether for dissertation. project or thesis. This book bui lds
   304→on shared experiences of good practice in research supervision at all levels

   308→The Good Supervisor intends to engage supervisors and students in a learning dialogue. It provides supervisors with research -informed suggestions
   309→about the dimensions of supervisory practice. that is systems and practices
   310→of working with conceptual frameworks, methods, processes of students'
   311→research, issues and practices of personal effectiveness in this long-term ,
   312→interpersonal interaction between supervisor and student. In its conception,

   314→supervisors and those supporting the development of students and supervisors alike, it has been absolutely crucial that the book should provide opportunities to engage readers in dialogue between research and experience,
